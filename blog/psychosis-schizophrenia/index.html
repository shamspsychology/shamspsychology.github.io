Psychosis & Schizophrenia

A medical condition that responds well to timely treatment

Shams Psychology Center team 30 September 2026

Overview

Psychosis involves a temporary or ongoing loss of contact with shared reality, including schizophrenia and related conditions, and is a medical condition that responds well to timely treatment.

Common Signs & Symptoms

  • Hallucinations, hearing, seeing, or sensing things others don't
  • Delusions, fixed false beliefs not shared by others
  • Disorganized thinking or speech
  • Social withdrawal or reduced motivation
  • Difficulty distinguishing between what is real and what is not
  • Marked change in personal functioning or self-care

When to Seek Help

Earlier treatment of psychosis is strongly linked to better long-term outcomes, please don't wait for a crisis to seek assessment.

Our Approach at Shams

Our psychiatrists provide careful diagnosis and medication management, combined with family psychoeducation and rehabilitation-focused support for daily functioning.

This page provides general information and is not a substitute for professional diagnosis. If you're concerned about yourself or someone you know, please reach out to schedule an assessment.
