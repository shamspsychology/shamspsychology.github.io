Substance Use & Addiction

A medical condition, not a moral failing

Shams Psychology Center team 30 September 2026

Overview

Substance use disorder involves a pattern of alcohol or drug use that causes significant impairment or distress, often alongside compulsive use despite harmful consequences.

Common Signs & Symptoms

  • Using more, or for longer, than intended
  • Unsuccessful efforts to cut down or stop
  • Cravings or strong urges to use
  • Continued use despite relationship, work, or health problems
  • Needing more of the substance for the same effect (tolerance)
  • Withdrawal symptoms when not using

When to Seek Help

Addiction is a medical condition, not a moral failing, reaching out for help is often the hardest and most important first step.

Our Approach at Shams

We provide structured assessment, medically informed detox referral where needed, individual and group therapy, and relapse-prevention planning grounded in CBT and DBT.

This page provides general information and is not a substitute for professional diagnosis. If you're concerned about yourself or someone you know, please reach out to schedule an assessment.
