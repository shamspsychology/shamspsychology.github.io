Why we're called Shams

Dr. Nabil Elkot founded the center in memory of his wife, the writer and screenwriter Nadine Shams, who died in 2014 after a medical error. That loss became a commitment: care here must be ethical, responsible and humane above all.

Questions we often hear before a first session

Don't see yours? Ask us directly.

Is what I say in a session kept confidential?

Yes. Confidentiality and safety are among our core values, and we protect the privacy of everything you share with us.

What is the difference between a psychiatrist and a psychologist?

A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who diagnoses and can prescribe medication when needed. A clinical psychologist provides talking therapy and assessments. They often work together on the same case.

I don't know which specialist I need. Where do I start?

Start with the free intro call. We listen to what you're going through, then suggest the specialist who best fits your needs.

Can I attend sessions online?

Yes. We offer online sessions as well as sessions at our Nozha and Downtown branches.

Do you work with children and teenagers?

Yes. We offer specialist assessment and therapy for children and teenagers, including ADHD and autism diagnosis, as well as support for families.

What should I do in an emergency?

We offer emergency consultations during opening hours; call us on 01200907001. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call an ambulance on 123 or go to the nearest emergency department.
